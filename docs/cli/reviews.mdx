---
title: Customer reviews
description: List, summarize and respond to App Store reviews.
---

```bash
appstore reviews list [--rating 1,2] [--territory USA,DEU] [--unanswered] [--sort -createdDate] [--limit 50] [--all]
appstore reviews get REVIEW_ID
appstore reviews summary [--max 500]
appstore reviews respond REVIEW_ID "Thanks!" --confirm
appstore reviews delete-response RESPONSE_ID --confirm
```

Territories use three-letter codes (`USA`, `GBR`, `DEU`, `JPN`). See [Respond to reviews](/guides/respond-to-reviews).
